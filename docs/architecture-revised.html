Revised VidQuery Architecture

The end-to-end pipeline redrawn with the retrieval topology corrected, the asynchronous job path made explicit, and the feedback loop and faithfulness gate promoted to real stages. Nine changes from the original figure are keyed below.

Figure 1 — Corrected end-to-end architecture

Teal marks everything that differs from the original: new nodes, control paths that carry no content, and the numbered badges that key into the change list below.

What changed, and why

1

/process returns a job id; /status reports progress

An ASR run on a one-hour video takes tens of minutes and a visual run takes several. A blocking POST times out in the browser long before either finishes. The sidebar's progress bar finally has something real to report.

2

BM25 indexes the chunker's output, not the embedder's

In the original figure BM25 hung off the Sentence-BERT node. BM25 scores term frequencies over tokenized text; it has no access to — and no use for — a 384-dimension vector.

3

RRF consumes both searches; it is not a third search

The original drew BM25, vector search and RRF as three peers on one row, all three arrowing into Gemini. As drawn, RRF had no inputs and nothing to fuse.

4

Click feedback re-enters at fusion, scaled to RRF's range

A rank-0 RRF score is 1/60 ≈ 0.0167, so the whole two-list score range tops out near 0.033. The document's 0.1 boost is roughly six times that range — one click would pin a chunk to rank 1 permanently. Cap it near 0.002, and disable it during evaluation or §12 stops being reproducible.

5

The faithfulness gate is a stage, not a footnote

BERTScore plus entity grounding runs after generation and produces the sidebar's confidence state — a real output. Use a smaller model than the default roberta-large, turn on baseline rescaling, and calibrate the threshold on your own dev set rather than asserting 0.75.

6

Pipelines fall back; they don't simply fail

Auto-captions can be empty or garbage, and Whisper hallucinates subtitle credits on silence. The dashed chain is the degradation path, and it terminates in an explicit "no extractable content" rather than a hallucinated transcript.

7

Index in the source language

Pushing 1,500+ caption segments per video through a free translation endpoint is slow, rate-limited and lossy. The multilingual encoder already handles cross-lingual retrieval; only the question needs translating, and only for BM25's benefit.

8

Two OCR engines, not three

trocr-base-printed is a single-line recognizer with no detection stage — hand it a full slide and it returns a hallucinated fragment. PaddleOCR and EasyOCR are both complete detect-then-recognize pipelines. Normalize case, whitespace and line order before voting, or agreement between two correct readings scores below your 0.7 gate and everything falls through to LLaVA.

9

faster-whisper replaces openai-whisper

Same model weights, CTranslate2 runtime, roughly 4–8× faster on CPU at int8 with equivalent accuracy. This is the single swap that moves the ASR path from "unusable on a laptop" to "usable on a laptop."

Two decisions the diagram can't make for you

  • The TrOCR slot. Figure 1 assumes you drop it for a two-engine ensemble. The alternative is keeping it behind PaddleOCR's DBNet detector and feeding it line crops — more faithful to the document, considerably more work, and slower. Pick before you write visual_pipeline.py.
  • The evaluation set. Thirty-two questions is too thin for a 0.71-against-0.57 Precision@4 claim to survive scrutiny. Target 80–120 with confidence intervals, and build the harness before the remaining features rather than after — every number in §12 is model- and chunking-dependent, and both are changing here.

Caption text for the thesis

Figure 1: Complete end-to-end VidQuery architecture. The Chrome Extension submits a video over HTTP and receives a job identifier immediately; a background worker reports stage progress back through /status. A smart detector routes the video to one of three extraction pipelines — captions, ASR, or visual OCR — with an explicit fallback chain when a pipeline yields no usable content. All three converge on a time-windowed chunker, whose output feeds a BM25 token index and a multilingual Sentence-BERT vector index in parallel. Both searches are merged by Reciprocal Rank Fusion, which additionally receives a bounded click-through boost derived from user interaction history. The four fused chunks form the sole context for Gemini, and the generated answer passes a faithfulness gate — BERTScore with entity grounding — before returning to the sidebar with its timestamp chips and confidence state.
